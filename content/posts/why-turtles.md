There's an old story about a lecture on the shape of the universe. At the end,
someone in the audience objects: the world is really a flat plate resting on
the back of a giant turtle. Asked what the turtle stands on, they have an
answer ready. It's turtles all the way down.

I've spent my career on the layers of software that other software stands on:
databases, cloud platforms, the plumbing in between. The joke lands a little
differently from down there. Every system I've worked on turned out to be
resting on another one, and most of the surprises came from a turtle I hadn't
looked at yet.

This blog is where I'll write about those layers and about the product work
of building on top of them.
