I'm [Gunnar Aasen](https://gunnaraasen.com/), a product manager who works on
cloud infrastructure and data platforms. This is where I write things down.

The name comes from the old story about the world resting on the back of a
turtle, which stands on another turtle, and so on: it's turtles all the way
down. Software is built the same way. Every layer sits on one below it, and
the interesting problems tend to show up where two layers meet.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/gunnaraasen/) and
[LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/gunnaraasen/), or follow along by
[RSS](/index.xml).
